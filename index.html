FAQs

Common Questions

How do I invest in AlSaqib projects?

You can reach out to our investment team at info@alsaqibinvestment.com or call us at +92 300 123 4567. We'll discuss your investment goals and explore suitable opportunities across our three divisions.

What's the minimum investment amount?

Investment minimums vary by project and division. Real estate projects typically start from PKR 5 million, while technology investments can range from PKR 2-10 million depending on the stake size.

How does AlSaqib ensure transparency?

Complete transparency is at the core of our operations. We provide regular financial reports, project updates, and maintain open communication channels with all stakeholders. Our ISO 27001 certification ensures data security.

What returns can I expect?

Returns vary based on project type and investment structure. Real estate projects typically offer 12-18% annual returns, while technology investments can deliver higher returns depending on growth. Industrial projects offer stable 10-15% returns.

Are there any hidden charges?

No. We believe in complete transparency. All charges, fees, and terms are clearly outlined in our investment agreements before you commit. What you see is what you get.

About Us

Our Story, Our Purpose

Founded with a vision to transform Pakistan's investment landscape, AlSaqib Investment has grown from a single-office startup to a multi-division enterprise driving real impact.

From Vision to Reality

AlSaqib Investment was established in 2015 in the heart of Lahore — DHA Phase 5. What began as a boutique real estate consultancy has evolved into a diversified investment group with three core divisions.

Our founder recognized that Pakistan's growing economy needed investment firms that didn't just allocate capital, but actively built and managed assets. This hands-on approach became our defining characteristic.

Today, with over 50 completed projects, 120 team members, and a presence across three sectors, we continue to push boundaries while staying true to our founding principles.

To be Pakistan's most trusted investment group, recognized for creating enduring value through innovation, integrity, and impact across every sector we touch.

Our Mission
